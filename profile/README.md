## What we do

**Custom software**
Web platforms, SaaS products, cross-platform desktop applications and developer tooling, from specification to production.

**AI-driven automation**
- Work assistants that track cases across ticket systems and prepare answers in advance
- Browser automation that agents drive and humans can watch live
- Hybrid scraping pipelines combining selectors and LLM extraction, turning websites into structured data

**Agentic AI and multi-agent systems**
Orchestrated agent teams with persistent context and human-in-the-loop control. Agent workflows are designed visually, connected to your tools through MCP, and run on cloud or self-hosted LLMs.

**SAP, end to end**
- **S/4HANA and ECC**: ABAP backend development
- **SAP BTP**: CAP services, Fiori and SAPUI5 applications, Angular front ends
- **SAP Integration Suite**: integration flows designed, tested and deployed
- **SAP SuccessFactors**: side-by-side extensions on BTP, and production support for SuccessFactors Learning
- **Production plumbing**: security, audit logging, central logging and on-premise connectivity

## How we work

- **Senior delivery.** The people you speak to are the people who build.
- **Quality by design.** Every change is specified, implemented, tested and reviewed, with a human approving each step.
- **Production-ready.** Security, logging and connectivity with your existing systems are part of the scope, not an afterthought.
- **AI-assisted engineering.** We use AI agents throughout the delivery cycle, from ABAP on S/4HANA to TypeScript or Rust. It keeps delivery fast without lowering quality, and an engineer remains accountable for every result.
